About the Book

When the pragmatic chipmunk privateer Captain Thorne steals a legendary artifact from the Sunken Temple, he doesn't just make an enemy of the tyrannical Rat King—he builds a family. Aboard the sturdy schooner The Mudskipper, Thorne assembles an unlikely crew of outcasts to outrun the warlord's heavy iron-clads: Sasha, a sugar glider who must unlock the Compass's ancient Sylvan magic; Elias, a gruff badger and seasoned helmsman; and Skulk, a massive weasel thug who finds redemption and an unexpected romantic bond with his new captain.

But the true fight for the archipelago has only just begun.

Nursing a ruined fleet, the Rat King forms a desperate, volatile alliance with the deadly syndicate leader Madame Viper. Hunted by pitch-black cutters and venomous polecat assassins, the crew is forced into uncharted, churning waters. From surviving psychological traps in fog-choked mangroves to navigating a gravity-defying oceanic trench, Thorne and Skulk must prove that their bond is their greatest weapon.

Caught in a massive three-way naval war, the crew of The Mudskipper will test the absolute limits of freedom and found family.

Ina Buko

Keti kapita Thorne, mawu-sidi fiti botamota, takapa poko-huna oda ata donawata doma-sato, mu na sinapa suwa-budi paba Mawu-bika mada solo—mu sinapa fami. Hina mota bota The Mudskipper, Thorne sinapa mabo pito diza para fasa iron-mota bika paba: Sasha, mawu suta ki nolyoto oda sylvan-magi Rini; Elias, bada rapi a bota-mota; a Skulk, fisi bika ki wiyoto kani a kamo nuwa faba kapita nuwa.

Baka, fai riti para lekaleka nawa rivapa solo.

Sifa paba Mawu-bika bera biki sina kani hika a mabo biki Madam Viper, keti ratu pisi dako a kora paka kifa mota fosa nwa dika lura bisi. Hika toko foke sike mido foza duko tura dona keri sika, Thorne a Skulk saki mu kamo kani biki hevi gida safi.

Hina bika te-we siko navo-fai, mota bota The Mudskipper boto masi tesi safi libe a fami sina.

Tesahoda Leno-
