Why Find A Spot?

Traditional park software is bloated, relies on perfect internet, and traps you in endless monthly SaaS subscriptions. We built something better.

1

Local-First Architecture

Campgrounds are in nature. Nature doesn't always have great Wi-Fi. Our software saves a local ledger to your device, meaning you can keep checking people in and running store sales even if your internet completely drops. It automatically syncs back to the cloud when you're back online.

2

Own Your Backend

Say goodbye to predatory SaaS models. We build your platform on top of Appwrite. You retain 100% ownership of your databases, guest files, and payment processing. No hidden fees based on your occupancy rates.

3

All-in-One Kiosk

Manage reservations, process ID checks, run the camp store Point of Sale (POS), print vehicle tags, and generate emergency souls checks all from a single, lightning-fast dashboard. Built for front desk speed.
